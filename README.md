# CMP 170HX: Silicon Gamble

Hunt for 64 GB of VRAM through the shadiest graphics card on the market.

Buy a CMP 170HX, spin the lottery, flash suspicious chips, and pray that there is a GPU inside instead of a brick. You might get 8 GB, keep a questionable 10 GB card, request a nearly fictional refund, or turn your purchase into electronic waste.

Reliable GPUs are also available here—which is exactly why you must not buy them. This is a pixel game about greed, chance, and making extremely bad decisions.
